Writing That Points Beyond the Page

Christian Author • Biblical Teacher

I write because I believe words can encourage people to stop, listen, examine Scripture, and draw closer to Jesus Christ.

My books move across biblical research, Apostolic and Pentecostal studies, Christian living, devotionals, healing, relationships, memoir, fiction, family resources, and ministry projects. The subjects change, but the purpose remains the same: to place Scripture before the reader and invite honest reflection.
