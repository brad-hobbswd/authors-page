WELCOME

Writing That Points Beyond the Page

My name is Bradley Hobbs, and I write because I believe words can encourage people to stop, listen, examine Scripture, and draw closer to Jesus Christ.

My writing explores faith, forgiveness, healing, spiritual growth, biblical truth, church life, relationships, identity, redemption, and the questions that often remain unspoken. Some books take the form of biblical studies and devotionals. Others tell stories through Christian fiction, memoir, and inspirational writing.

Although the subjects may differ, the purpose remains consistent. I want every book to encourage readers to open Scripture, consider what it says, and allow the truth of God's Word to speak into real life.

FEATURED BOOK

Who Made You Judge?

Rediscovering Truth Without Condemnation

Who Made You Judge? invites readers to examine one of the difficult questions raised by the teachings of Jesus. It is a call to look inward before looking outward and to understand how truth, mercy, grace, and personal responsibility belong together.

Through Scripture, reflection, and practical application, the book challenges readers to pursue conviction without pride, grace without compromise, and love without abandoning truth.

“Who made you judge?” is more than a question. It is an invitation to let Jesus examine our hearts before we examine someone else's.

FEATURED FICTION

The Substitute

A Story of Brokenness, Redemption, and Hope

What begins as a story about addiction becomes something much deeper. The Substitute explores brokenness, shame, difficult choices, forgiveness, and the longing to be seen, loved, and restored.

Through honest storytelling and unforgettable characters, the novel explores the reality that redemption is never beyond God's reach and that grace can transform even the darkest chapters of a person's life.

“Sometimes addiction is not the problem. Sometimes addiction is the substitute.”

FEATURED ENCOURAGEMENT

No Weapon

Standing on the Promises of God

Inspired by the promise of Isaiah 54:17, No Weapon encourages believers to stand confidently upon God's faithfulness during seasons of uncertainty, fear, spiritual warfare, and opposition.

Rather than focusing on the size of the battle, this book turns attention toward the character of the God whose promises remain faithful.

“No weapon formed against you shall prosper.” Isaiah 54:17

WHY I WRITE

Scripture First. Real Life. Real Hope.

Every book begins with prayer, Scripture, a question, a burden, or a story that refuses to let go.

I write about real life because faith is not lived in perfect circumstances. People experience grief, disappointment, fear, forgiveness, failure, recovery, questions, relationships, church experiences, and seasons when God's presence can seem difficult to see.

My desire is to meet those realities with Scripture and to remind readers that the Word of God still speaks into the places where life hurts, faith is tested, and hope feels distant.

The subjects may change from one book to another, but the purpose remains the same: to encourage faith, challenge spiritual growth, and point readers toward Jesus Christ.
