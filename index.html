BRAD HOBBS

Writing That Points Beyond the Page

Christian Author • Biblical Teacher

I write because I believe words can encourage people to stop, listen, examine Scripture, and draw closer to Jesus Christ.

My completed books and published studies now span biblical research, Apostolic and Pentecostal teaching, Christian living, devotionals, healing, relationships, memoir, fiction, and family stories. Each work reflects a different part of the journey, but the purpose remains the same: to place truth before the reader, invite honest reflection, and point hearts toward Jesus Christ.

This site highlights selected completed works. The Library brings together the broader collection, including individual books, connected series, and ministry studies.
