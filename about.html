A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Faith, Scripture, and the Stories We Carry

I am Bradley Hobbs, a Christian author and biblical teacher who believes that Scripture belongs in the middle of real life.

People do not live their lives in neat chapters. We carry questions, memories, relationships, disappointments, victories, wounds, hopes, responsibilities, and moments that change us. Faith has to meet us there.

That conviction has shaped the way I write. Whether I am developing a Bible study, writing a devotional, exploring a difficult question, or creating a fictional story, I want the work to connect biblical truth with the realities people actually experience.

My writing is not intended to replace Scripture. It is intended to send the reader back to it.

MY APPROACH

Scripture Comes First

Every subject does not require the same kind of book, but every Christian book should have a reason for existing.

01

Scripture

The Bible provides the foundation. My goal is to encourage readers to engage Scripture directly, examine its meaning, and allow God's Word to challenge and encourage them.

02

Honesty

Real people face real questions. I believe Christian writing should be willing to address difficult subjects honestly rather than hiding behind comfortable language.

03

Grace

Truth and grace should not be enemies. My writing seeks to examine difficult subjects while remembering the compassion, mercy, and restoration found in Jesus Christ.

04

Transformation

Information alone is not the goal. I want readers to encounter Scripture in a way that encourages deeper faith, honest reflection, spiritual growth, and changed lives.

WHY I WRITE

Because Someone May Need the Words

I have learned that a book does not have to answer every question to be meaningful. Sometimes a book simply needs to ask the question someone has been afraid to ask.

Sometimes someone needs to hear that they are not forgotten. Sometimes they need permission to examine what they believe. Sometimes they need encouragement to forgive. Sometimes they need to understand that failure does not have to be the final chapter.

And sometimes someone simply needs to be reminded that Jesus Christ is still worth knowing.

That is the heart behind my writing.

“
I do not want my books to become the destination. I want them to become an invitation to open Scripture, seek truth, grow in faith, and know Jesus Christ more deeply.
