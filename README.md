# Brad Hobbs | Christian Author & Biblical Teacher

Official author website for Bradley (Brad) Hobbs, featuring Christian books, biblical teaching, Apostolic and Pentecostal studies, devotionals, fiction, memoir, Christian living, personal stories, and ministry resources.

**Live website:** https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/authors-page/  
**Library:** https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/authors-page/library.html

## Website Overview

The homepage (`index.html`) introduces Brad Hobbs and highlights selected publications, teaching resources, connected book series, standalone works, and ministry projects. The complete collection is organized in the Library.

### Homepage Sections

- **Biblical Teaching:** `I Refuse to Stay Bound`, `Beyond the Veil`, and `The Seven Things`.
- **Christian Living & Discipleship:** `You Have Not Because You Ask Not` and `The Grace of Asking`.
- **Personal Story:** `From the Bottom to Grace`.
- **Author Introduction:** An overview of Brad's writing and the purpose of the website.
- **New Biblical Studies:** `Sodom and Gomorrah` and `The Offering God Can Accept`.
- **The Who Question Series:** Three books presented together:
  1. `Who Made You Judge?`
  2. `Who Gave You Authority?`
  3. `Who Do You Call, Savior?`
- **Related Standalone Work:** `Who Are You to Call Unclean? When God Has Made It Holy`. This is a separate book, not Book Four of the Who Question Series. It is positioned near the series because its themes connect with questions of judgment, tradition, belonging, and biblical truth.
- **Personal Experience & Spiritual Examination:** `SELF-DECEIVED: When Religious Knowledge Replaces Obedience`.
- **Relationships & Understanding:** `The Other Side of the Story` and `The Other Side of the Story: LGBTQIA+ Edition`.
- **Support the Writing:** Options for readers to support ongoing books, studies, and ministry projects.
